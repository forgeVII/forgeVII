# Harish Chand — forgeVII

**Systems integration, hardware test and physical prototyping. I turn ideas into working machines — then find out where they break.**

I build integrated hardware: mechanical construction, power, wiring, sensors and firmware brought together on the bench. Firmware is AI-assisted; the engineering is mine — architecture, integration, probing, calibration, field testing and failure diagnosis.

## What I actually do

- **Integration** — mechanical, electrical and firmware pieces brought together into one working system.
- **Bench diagnostics** — multimeter, logic analyzer, protocol sniffing (UART / I2C / SPI), power-rail and ground-fault isolation.
- **Power and wiring** — buck converters, BMS / lithium safety, star grounding, harnesses, header maps.
- **Field testing** — flight-sim and real-game validation, fault injection, failure diagnosis.
- **Documentation** — as-built notes, safety rules, bring-up faults and the fixes that worked.

> **Builder of working things, not promises.**
